## Risk Assessment

### Technical Risks

#### High Risk
1. **Scalability Issues**
   - *Risk:* Application doesn't scale beyond 10K users
   - *Mitigation:* Load testing, CDN, database optimization
   - *Contingency:* Cloud infrastructure upgrade plan

2. **Data Loss**
   - *Risk:* User data or content lost due to system failure
   - *Mitigation:* Automated backups, redundant storage
   - *Contingency:* Disaster recovery plan, insurance

#### Medium Risk
3. **Browser Compatibility**
   - *Risk:* Features break in different browsers
   - *Mitigation:* Cross-browser testing, progressive enhancement
   - *Contingency:* Graceful degradation strategy

4. **API Changes**
   - *Risk:* Streamlit or dependencies break functionality
   - *Mitigation:* Version pinning, regular updates, monitoring
   - *Contingency:* Migration plan to alternative frameworks

### Business Risks

#### High Risk
5. **Funding Sustainability**
   - *Risk:* Unable to secure ongoing funding
   - *Mitigation:* Diversified revenue streams, grants, donations
   - *Contingency:* Reduce scope, volunteer model

6. **Content Rights**
   - *Risk:* Copyright issues with audio/visual content
   - *Mitigation:* Use only licensed/public domain content, legal review
   - *Contingency:* Content audit, removal plan

#### Medium Risk
7. **Community Management**
   - *Risk:* Toxic community or low engagement
   - *Mitigation:* Clear guidelines, active moderation, community managers
   - *Contingency:* Community restructuring plan

8. **Competition**
   - *Risk:* Similar platforms emerge and capture market share
   - *Mitigation:* Focus on quality, accessibility, community
   - *Contingency:* Differentiation strategy, partnership opportunities

### Cultural/Social Risks

#### Medium Risk
9. **Cultural Sensitivity**
   - *Risk:* Content misrepresents or offends cultural groups
   - *Mitigation:* Cultural advisors, sensitivity review, community input
   - *Contingency:* Rapid response team, content revision process

10. **Accessibility Compliance**
    - *Risk:* Fail to meet accessibility standards
    - *Mitigation:* Regular audits, user testing, expert consultation
    - *Contingency:* Remediation plan, legal compliance team

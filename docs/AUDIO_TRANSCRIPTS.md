## About Audio Assets

### Audio File Organization

Audio files are organized in the following structure:

```
public/assets/audio/
├── fadein/              # Gita Scroll narrative audio with fade-in
├── ambient/             # Ambient soundscapes (instrumental)
├── composite/           # Fall of Dharma composite mixes
├── forest/              # Weapon Quest audio organized by story
│   ├── forest_of_austerity/
│   ├── shiva_and_the_hunter/
│   ├── celestial_audience/
│   └── trial_of_heaven/
└── karna/               # Trials of Karna audio organized by story
    ├── suns_gift/
    ├── brahmin_curse/
    ├── friends_vow/
    ├── birth_revealed/
    └── final_arrow/
```

### Ambient Soundscapes

Ambient audio tracks are instrumental soundscapes without spoken content. They provide atmospheric background music to enhance the storytelling experience but do not require transcripts as they contain no narrative information.

### Accessibility Notes

- **All narrative audio has corresponding text transcripts** (this document)
- **Text content is always visible on the story page** alongside audio controls
- **Audio transcripts match the narrative meditation text** displayed on each story page
- **Users can read the text without playing audio** to access the full content
- **Audio files use standard HTML5 audio controls** which are keyboard accessible

### Audio Playback

- All audio uses native HTML5 `<audio>` elements
- Audio controls are keyboard accessible
- Users can play, pause, adjust volume, and scrub through audio
- No audio auto-plays; user action is required
- Ambient tracks default to looping for continuous background atmosphere
